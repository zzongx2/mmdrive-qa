# Towards Radar-Aware VLM-Based Autonomous Driving
## via mmWave Radar-Vision-Language Reasoning

**Manuscript under review**

We introduce mmDrive-QA, a driving question-answering benchmark,
and mmPointRet, a question-guided radar point retriever,
to study how vision-language models use mmWave radar evidence.
